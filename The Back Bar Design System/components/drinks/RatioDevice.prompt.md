Every recipe detail view leads with this — the template, not the recipe.

```jsx
<RatioDevice parts={[{value:"2",label:"Spirit"},{value:"¾",label:"Citrus"},{value:"¾",label:"Sweet"}]} />
```

2–4 parts, custom labels. Numerals are brass display Caslon; labels are caps utility at 0.32em tracking. Use `size="md"` inside cards and `sm` in dense lists — never below 20px numerals.
